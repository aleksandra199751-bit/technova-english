04 / Reading

GreenMart: Before the Pilot

Read Emma’s project update to the TechNova team. Identify the main purpose, then read again for details and complete the meeting notes.

Team update · GreenMart pilot

Goals, risks & next steps

Hi team, Before tomorrow’s GreenMart meeting, I’d like to confirm our goals and flag a risk.

Our objective is to make the new customer portal easier to use. We are using an OKR to track progress. Our first key result is to increase the percentage of pilot users who can track an order without help from 70% to 90% by 30 June. We will measure this in a test with 20 users. That means at least 18 users need to complete the task without help. The test team has confirmed that this target is achievable.

Please remember our Definition of Done. The order-tracking feature must pass the agreed tests, have no critical defects, and include updated help instructions. Meeting these quality standards does not automatically mean we have reached our user target. We need to check both.

There is one important assumption: we expect GreenMart to send the test accounts by Tuesday. They have not confirmed this yet. Our main constraint is the pilot budget. It is fixed at £5,000, so we cannot simply pay for extra support.

Daniel has raised a concern about the accounts. If they arrive late, the user test could be delayed by two days. Our risk assessment puts the likelihood at medium and the impact at high. I have added the risk to the risk register, and Daniel will be its owner.

Our mitigation strategy is to request 5 sample accounts on Monday and check them immediately. This should help us discover access problems early. Our contingency plan is different: if the full set is still missing at noon on Tuesday, we will use approved training accounts for the first test session. That is our backup plan. Daniel will check that those accounts are ready before Tuesday.

We also have a current issue. The password-reset page is already showing an error. This is not just something that might happen. I have recorded it in the issue log, and Priya is working on a fix. She will update us by 3 p.m. today.

Please keep GreenMart’s project lead, Julia, in the loop. I will send her an update after our Monday account check. If you identify another risk before then, tell me early. Do not wait for the next weekly meeting.
