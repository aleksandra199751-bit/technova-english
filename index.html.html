03 / Reading

A New Project at TechNova

↗

On Monday morning, the TechNova team met to start a new project for GreenMart, a growing retail company. GreenMart wants to replace its old customer portal because customers often have problems checking their orders and contacting support.

At the kick-off meeting, project manager Emma Lewis presented the project charter and went through the main requirements with the team. The current scope includes a new login system, customer profiles, order tracking and an online support page. Online payments are not part of the project at this stage.

Several stakeholders joined the meeting, including GreenMart’s operations manager, two customer service representatives and TechNova’s development lead. They agreed that the first deliverable would be a working prototype. The first major milestone is getting the client’s approval of that prototype within six weeks.

The timeline is quite tight, so Emma wants to keep the project close to its original baseline. However, two days after the meeting, GreenMart asked about adding a live-chat feature. Emma explained that this was a new requirement and asked the client to submit a change request. The team now needs to understand whether the feature will affect the deadline.

For now, GreenMart still needs to approve the final requirements and give its sign-off. Emma will also send a short status update every Friday so that everyone knows what has been completed and what happens next.
